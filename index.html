Privacy Policy for Artha

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE_DATE]

Artha (Android package: com.safi.artha) is provided by Md Sahin. For privacy questions, contact umshahinsafi@gmail.com.

01

Information used on your device

Artha is an offline personal finance app. It stores and processes the information you enter on your device, including account names and balances, income and expenses, transfers, categories, notes, budgets, monthly plans, recurring entries, investment records and manual valuations. It also stores app preferences, local record identifiers, installation metadata and operational information needed for backups, reminders and app locking.

This information supports the app's recordkeeping, calculations, organization, reminders, backup and restore features. Artha does not require registration or an online user account. The app does not transmit these records to the developer or to an Artha server.

02

Analytics, advertising and sharing

Artha does not include advertising, analytics, automatic crash-upload services, bank connections or automatic cloud synchronization. The developer does not receive or sell your in-app financial records. The app does not request Internet access.

03

Backups and file access

You can export a backup or select a backup to restore using Android's file picker. Artha accesses the document you select for that operation. Backups contain financial records and app preferences and should be treated as sensitive.

You choose where to save a backup. If you select a cloud storage or other external document provider, that provider may store or transmit the file under its own terms and privacy policy. Artha does not operate that service. Copies you export are outside Artha's private storage and remain under your control.

Backup password encryption is optional. When enabled, the backup uses AES-256-GCM encryption. An unencrypted backup can be read by anyone who obtains it. Keep your backup password safe; the developer cannot recover it. App-lock PIN verification material and biometric templates are not included in financial backups.

04

App locking and device security

You may enable a local app PIN and biometric unlock on supported devices. Artha stores protected PIN verification material locally using Android Keystore-backed protection. Android handles biometric authentication; Artha receives an authentication result and does not access or store your fingerprint or face templates.

App data is stored in Android's app-private storage. App locking is an access control and is not a claim that the entire financial database is separately encrypted by Artha. Security also depends on your device, operating system and control of exported files.

05

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Artha can display local budget alerts and recurring-entry review reminders. You can manage notification permission in Android settings. Reminder delivery depends on Android background scheduling and device settings.

06

Retention and deletion

Artha retains local records while they remain in its storage. To remove all Artha local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Artha > Storage (or Storage & cache) > Clear storage (or Clear data), or uninstall Artha. Menu names vary by device. Clearing storage removes local records, settings and app-lock information.

Exported backups are not deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. Delete those files separately from each location where you saved or copied them, including any cloud provider's trash or retained copies according to that provider's controls. Restoring a backup brings its records back into Artha.

Artha is configured to disable Android automatic app backup and exclude app data from device transfer. Keep your own backup if you want to restore your records. The developer has no server copy of your in-app records to recover or delete. If you forget your app PIN, recovery requires clearing local app data and restoring a trusted backup, if available.

07

Support communications

If you contact the support email, the developer receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request. Email is processed by the email providers involved. Do not send PINs, passwords or financial backup files.

Children

Artha is intended for personal finance recordkeeping and is not specifically designed for children. It does not ask users to provide their age. Any target-audience declaration in Google Play must reflect the publisher's actual intended audience.
